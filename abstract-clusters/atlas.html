Study types are provisional. Action markers identify wording in abstracts, not completed experiments or evidence quality. Unknown means unreported or undetected in the abstract.

How to interpret these results

Four study types are predefined, not discovered by clustering. When multiple types match, provisional priority is engineering → methods → mechanism → clinical. Other matches remain secondary labels. Unresolved and review records are retained separately.

The matrix uses existing abstract similarity values, with organ and microbial names downweighted. The Greys scale spans cosine similarity 0–1, from white for lower similarity to dark gray for higher similarity. Negative values are displayed at 0. Similarity does not establish novelty, duplication or intellectual lineage.

Lexical rules can mistake background, negation or cited work for performed actions. Read the source sentences before interpreting any marker.
